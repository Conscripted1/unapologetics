Before You Begin

The Five Frames

Each frame describes the posture you take toward a statement. Ask: what does my honest gut say — and what would that response require of me?

bolt

Essentials

I agree, and it's central.

I hold this as core to the faith. If another believer disagreed, I'd lovingly contend for it — not to win, but because their understanding of God's nature depends on it. To be wrong here is to worship a different God.

close

Lies

I disagree, and it must be named.

This statement misrepresents who God is. For the sake of the church's unity in truth, I'd feel compelled to refute it and replace it with God's truth.

≈

Non-Essentials

I lean, but hold it loosely.

I have an opinion, but Scripture isn't decisive here, and I can see faithful Christians landing differently. Where a position draws someone closer to God, grace is the right response.

balance

Wisdom

It depends.

The right answer is reached by applying biblical wisdom to a specific people, season, or situation — and it may not be the same for everyone. This calls for the Word, the Spirit, and wise counsel rather than a fixed verdict.

warning

Unclean

Neither yes nor no is safe.

Both answers, as the statement is worded, misrepresent God — so the statement itself must be reframed. Left as-is, it pulls well-meaning Christians into disharmony that doesn't glorify God.

Wisdom vs. Unclean: Wisdom says the answer varies by context. Unclean says the question is malformed and must be rebuilt before it can be answered at all.
